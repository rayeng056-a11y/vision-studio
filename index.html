Notre Histoire

A Taste of Paris,
Born in Tunis

Pâtisserie Madeleine et Proust is more than a bakery — it is a sensory journey. Named after Proust's famous madeleine, the little cake that unlocked a universe of memory, we believe every bite should transport you.

Founded in the heart of Tunis, our artisans draw from classic French baking traditions to craft desserts of extraordinary refinement — viennoiseries, entremets, custom celebration cakes, and gourmet treats — using only the finest ingredients.

Premium Ingredients

Imported French butter, Valrhona chocolate & fresh local produce.

Handcrafted Daily

Every piece baked fresh each morning with artisan precision.

Culinary Excellence

Recipes inspired by the finest pâtisseries of Paris & Lyon.
